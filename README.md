# Partner Field Guide

An interactive, single-page learning app about AWS from a partner company's point of view, for someone starting out in video, AdminOps (QuickBooks) and RevOps and growing to an advanced level.

Open `index.html` in any browser. No install or build step needed.

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | What's inside |
|---|---------|---------------|
| 00 | Start here | Learning levels, the partner business loop, 90-day checklist (saved in your browser) |
| 01 | Cloud & AWS basics | Regions/AZs/edge diagram, shared responsibility toggle, pricing models, Well-Architected pillars, certification path |
| 02 | Service explorer | ~57 services with plain-English descriptions, search and category filters |
| 03 | Partner Network | Partner Paths, tier ladder, programs (Specializations, MSP, MAP, ISV Accelerate, Marketplace) |
| 04 | Portals & programs | Partner Central (APC/APN Portal), ACE, APFP, AMMP, PSF, Skill Builder, certifications, Marketing Central, Well-Architected Tool, billing, CRM connector; deal journey across portals; lead sources; partnership types; certifications |
| 05 | Who's who & RACI | AWS roles (PDM, PSA, PMM...), your company's roles, customer roles, what each side gives and gets, RACI matrix |
| 06 | GTM & co-sell | ACE stages, co-sell motions, MAP phases, sales plays |
| 07 | Funding programs | Funding request-to-payout flow, program table, rejection reasons |
| 08 | RevOps | Lead-to-cash, weighted pipeline calculator, metrics, systems map |
| 09 | AdminOps & QuickBooks | Invoice lifecycle, revenue types, Marketplace payout and DSO calculators, month-end close checklist |
| 10 | Video & content | Production pipeline, content formats, brand/trademark do's and don'ts, measurement |
| 11 | Calendar & history | Typical AWS partner year, milestones, AWS revenue chart |
| 12 | Glossary | 80+ searchable terms |
| 13 | Quiz | 14 questions |
| 14 | Grow & contribute | 30-60-90 plan, use cases and strategies, rules, habits, impact metrics, learning links |

## Editing

The app source is `app/partner-field-guide.html` (content is in plain JavaScript arrays near the top of the script). After editing, run `./build.sh` to regenerate `index.html`.

AWS program names, tier thresholds, fees and funding rules change often. Treat the app as a map and confirm specifics in AWS Partner Central.
